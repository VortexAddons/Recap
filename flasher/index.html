Flash Recapper

Install the firmware on your ESP32 straight from the browser. No toolchain needed.

Before you start

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Safari and Firefox can't flash over USB).
  2. Plug the ESP32 into your computer with a data-capable USB cable.
  3. If the board isn't listed in the next step, install the CP210x or CH340 USB driver for it.
This browser can't flash devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs HTTPS or localhost. Open this page from the address it was published at.

After flashing

  1. Open the Recap app on your Android phone and tap Recapper, then Set up.
  2. Follow the prompts: pair, say a test phrase, pick your WiFi.
  3. To redo setup later, hold the button while plugging in the ESP32 until setup mode starts (about 3 seconds).

Wiring: INMP441 WS to GPIO25, SCK to GPIO26, SD to GPIO33, L/R to GND, VDD to 3.3V, GND to GND. Button between GPIO14 and GND.
